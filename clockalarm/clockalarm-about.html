ClockAlarm — About

A modern clock, alarm, weather and world time widget for your Windows desktop. Free, ad-free, no telemetry.

Overview

ClockAlarm is a lightweight desktop widget that puts a beautiful clock, alarms, weather forecast, world time, and countdown events directly on your screen. It floats unobtrusively on your desktop, resizes to fit your layout, and adapts natively to your Windows Light or Dark Mode theme.

No ads. No telemetry. No background services required. The free tier is fully functional — Pro unlocks unlimited alarms, advanced repeating schedules, custom sounds, custom date formats, hex colour theming, a full World Clock tool, and dedicated Stopwatch & Timer windows.
